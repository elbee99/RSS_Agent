The grading rubric actually used lives in `RUBRIC` in `scripts/rank_articles.py`.
Edit it there to change what gets picked. Summary:

Want (high score):
- Chemical / spectroscopic imaging of batteries (X-ray, neutron, Raman/IR, NMR/MRI, cryo-EM/EELS/4D-STEM, ToF-SIMS, optical)
- Operando / in situ techniques, especially new methods
- Spatial and multiscale heterogeneity, tomography
- Real mechanistic understanding backed by direct evidence
- Genuine breakthroughs in high-impact journals

Don't want (low score):
- "Sandwich papers": changed X / doped with Y / added Z, ~10% better, hand-wavy justification
- Non-battery topics (PV, catalysis, fuel cells, TENGs, supercapacitors)
