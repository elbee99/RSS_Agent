## 1. [Four-dimensional X-ray nano-holotomography reveals multiscale chemomechanics in composite graphite–silicon battery electrodes](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41565-026-02327-6)

**9/10** · *Nature Nanotechnology* · operando 4D X-ray nano-holotomography

Particle-resolved time-lapse nano-holotomography on composite graphite–silicon electrodes links local ionic transport pathways to electrochemical activity and reversibility. This gives multiscale chemomechanical heterogeneity (particle-to-particle, network-level) in a commercially relevant composite. Only the abstract teaser was available, so quantitative details are unverified.

## 3. [Stage‐Specific Pressure Programming Decouples Low‐Pressure Sulfur Reduction From High‐Pressure Interfacial Reconstruction in All‐Solid‐State Lithium–Sulfur Batteries](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.75779?af=R)

**6/10** · *Advanced Materials* · state-resolved VSANS, operando pressure/electrochemistry

Shows that stack pressure acts stage-specifically in all-solid-state Li–S cells. Low-pressure cycling fills pores and accumulates inactive LiXS. State-resolved VSANS finds a \~5 nm feature that persists after 1 MPa discharge and disappears on high-pressure charge. This led to a 1 MPa discharge / 180 MPa charge protocol with 98.8% retention over 100 cycles. Mechanistic evidence is partly indirect.

## 4. [Transition-Metal-Mediated Discharge Intermediates Set Anionic-Redox Potentials and Voltage Hysteresis in Li-Rich Cathodes](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c17652)

**6/10** · *Journal of the American Chemical Society* · electrochemical relaxation analysis + synchrotron XAS/RIXS

Claims first experimental evidence of a metastable Ni(2-δ)+/(O–O)n− discharge intermediate (MLCT) in a DRX Li2Ni0.33Ta0.66O3, showing relaxation of this intermediate drives voltage hysteresis. Ru substitution then shifts O–O reduction potential upward. The mechanism is interesting, but the evidence is bulk spectroscopy, not spatially resolved.

## 5. [Contouring mitigates voiding at the lithium/ceramic electrolyte interface on discharging solid-state batteries](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41560-026-02172-w)

**6/10** · *Nature Energy* · patterned Li/ceramic electrolyte interface; electrochemical stripping tests

A geometric "egg-box" contoured Li/ceramic interface raises the critical discharge current density for void formation by about 10x. It also supports EV-like discharge profiles without polarization. This addresses a key failure mode in Li-metal solid-state batteries. The abstract doesn't say whether the mechanism was confirmed by direct imaging or by modelling.
