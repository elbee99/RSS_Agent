## 1. [Resolving the Structural Origin of Lattice Oxygen Release across Scales in Practical Lithium‐Manganese‐Rich Electrodes](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/aenm.71746?af=R)

**8/10** · *Advanced Energy Materials* · OEMS + multiscale synchrotron X-ray spectro-microscopy (XANES mapping) + STEM/EELS

Maps Mn reduction as a fingerprint of O2 release from electrode to grain scale (four orders of magnitude). It finds no electrode- or particle-scale gradient. Frontal (Li-intercalation-plane) facets reduce more deeply, and normalizing by frontal-facet area reconciles the twofold difference in O2 evolution between the two electrodes. This gives a quantitative structural descriptor.

## 2. [Discovering Underpotentially Deposited Crystalline Sodium Metal Nanoparticles on the Surface of Hard Carbon Anodes](https://pubs.acs.org/jacsat/article/doi/10.1021/jacs.6c11841/5459442/Discovering-Underpotentially-Deposited-Crystalline)

**8/10** · *Journal of the American Chemical Society ASAP* · cryo-EM + cryo-electron tomography (EELS/diffraction)

Cryo-ET localizes crystalline Na metal clusters in sodiated hard carbon in 3D, finding them on or near particle surfaces rather than in closed bulk nanopores. This contradicts the prevailing pore-filling picture and redirects mitigation of underpotential plating toward particle surfaces and interfaces.

## 3. [Monitoring Lithiation Gradients in Solid-State Batteries Using \<em\>Operando\</em\> Neutron Radiography](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405829726007476?dgcid=rss_sd_all)

**7/10** · *Energy Storage Materials* · operando neutron radiography

Operando neutron radiography is used to map lithiation gradients across solid-state battery electrodes, exploiting the strong neutron contrast of Li. This is a relevant, uncommon operando imaging method for SSBs. The abstract is missing, so quantitative findings can't be judged.

## 4. [Operando X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy Reveals Oxide Solid Electrolyte Stability against Electrochemically Plated Lithium](https://pubs.acs.org/aelccp/article/doi/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02717/5458706/Operando-X-ray-Photoelectron-Spectroscopy-Reveals)

**7/10** · *ACS Energy Letters ASAP* · lab-based operando XPS with electrochemical Li plating

Plates Li electrochemically inside the XPS, avoiding the artefacts of evaporation or sputtering. It shows LATP is reduced to a Li+/Ti3+ mixed-conducting interphase that partly relaxes, while LLZO shows no resolvable Zr or La reduction. This resolves a known inconsistency in the in situ XPS literature.

## 5. [Operando Magnetometry Decodes Space‐Charge Storage in the Solid‐Electrolyte Interphase](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.74811?af=R)

**7/10** · *Advanced Materials* · operando magnetometry, operando AP-XPS

Uses operando magnetometry with Fe3C as a magnetic probe to follow reversible charge storage at the SEI/electrode interface. Operando AP-XPS supports an SEI-centred space-charge mechanism, giving about 236 mAh/g extra capacity. The method is new and transferable, but space-charge storage claims in conversion-type electrodes are still debated and the evidence is indirect.

## 6. [Demonstrating the Generality of Kerr-Gated Raman Spectroscopy Measurements of High Charge States in Graphitic Electrodes for Li-Ion Batteries](https://pubs.acs.org/aaemcq/article/doi/10.1021/acsaem.6c02125/5458705/Demonstrating-the-Generality-of-Kerr-Gated-Raman)

**6/10** · *ACS Applied Energy Materials ASAP* · operando Kerr-gated Raman spectroscopy

Kerr-gating suppresses fluorescence and lets the graphite G-band be resolved at high lithiation (stage 2→1, 0.5≤x≤1), where conventional Raman fails. The paper shows the G-band shift tracks state of lithiation across graphite sources, carbonate and ionic-liquid electrolytes, and 10 cycles. The work is a generality demonstration, not spatial imaging.

## 7. [Operando Raman Spectroscopy-Guided 24-Hour Aging Assessment of Activated Carbon Positive Electrodes for Lithium-Ion Capacitors](https://pubs.acs.org/aelccp/article/doi/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02322/5459791/Operando-Raman-Spectroscopy-Guided-24-Hour-Aging)

**6/10** · *ACS Energy Letters ASAP* · operando Raman spectroscopy (D1 band analysis)

Operando Raman of AC cathodes in lithium-ion capacitors under high-voltage cycling finds a turning point in D1 band width. It narrows first as CEI and gas passivate defects, then broadens with corrosion. An earlier turning point correlates with faster capacity decay, giving a 24-h aging diagnostic. The work is spectroscopic but not spatially resolved.

## 8. [In Situ Visualizing the Electric‐Field‐Driven Assembly of Gradient Carbon Dot Hydrogel Electrolytes for Stable Zinc Battery](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.74436?af=R)

**6/10** · *Advanced Materials* · operando electrochemical–confocal fluorescence microscopy

Couples a confocal fluorescence platform with an electrochemical cell to track fluorescent carbon dots migrating electrophoretically in a hydrogel electrolyte and enriching at the Zn interface. The visualization is used to build a gradient electrolyte. The mechanism rationale still leans on FEM and DFT, and the additive is zinc-specific.

## 9. [Electrochemically In Situ Formed Inverse‐Honeycomb Local Ordering for Low‐Voltage‐Hysteresis Oxygen Redox in Na/Li Layered Oxide Cathodes](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/anie.2928007?af=R)

**6/10** · *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* · atomic-resolution STEM, ex situ spectroscopy, thermal relaxation, ion-exchange

Atomic-resolution STEM shows that deep desodiation of NaLi0.2Ni0.2Mn0.6O2 forms an inverse-honeycomb superstructure in the alkali layer. Comparing Li analogues made by ion exchange from pristine and charged precursors, plus thermal relaxation and spectroscopy, ties this ordering to suppressed Ni-centred ligand-to-metal charge transfer and lower voltage hysteresis. The evidence is mostly ex situ.
